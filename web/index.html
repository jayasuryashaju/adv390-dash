    Speed

    Hold a steady speed (riding, or spinning the rear wheel on a stand), read the bike's speedometer, and enter it here. Uses the rear wheel.

    Lean

    Hold the bike upright and set zero. Then lean it against a known angle (a phone level works) and enter that angle.

    Front brake

    Press the button, then squeeze the lever as hard as you would in an emergency stop for 5 seconds. The hardest squeeze becomes 100 %.

    Rear brake

    Release both brakes for a moment so the resting level is learned. Then press the button and push the pedal as hard as in an emergency stop for 5 seconds.

    Fuel

    Fill the tank to the brim and reset Trip 2. Ride with the dashboard connected the whole time. At the next brim fill-up, enter what the pump shows.

    Shift light

    Two stages, like the KTM: an orange frame as you near the shift point, then the whole screen turns orange when it is time to shift. Works in every ride mode.

    Cluster themes
